# Visual Direction and Supplementary Thinking

> Historical baseline (2026-09-10): the product is now named Lingxi, and the user requires real-time 3D as the goal. The latest decisions are in the [real-time 3D decision](decisions/001-realtime-desktop.md), [brand design](08-brand-and-design-system.md), and [extension architecture](09-extension-architecture.md). Older recommendations that conflict with the new decisions no longer apply.

Source: written analysis and a concept board the user added on 2026-09-10. This document is an extracted record, not a verbatim transcript. Suggestions inside the materials to generate images, start modeling, and so on are kept as discussion. They are not treated as instructions to execute this round.

## 1. What the new material added

The core aesthetic can be stated as: **a comfortable, quiet, fluffy kitten, as if it just happens to live beside you.** What a user might be willing to watch for a long time is its natural resting state, not only the moments when it plays up being cute. The "might" matters: the material's analysis of why it would be chosen is a design hypothesis, not a verified conclusion about an individual's psychology.

The new material cites preference analysis for numbered cats such as 2, 4, 6, 9, and 16, 24–32, but does not provide the matching complete numbered sheet, so each number's appearance and ranking cannot be checked from it. The figure attached this round is one Peipei product concept board. It is not treated as that numbered sheet.

![Peipei product concept board](../source/peipei-concept-board.png)

## 2. What can be observed directly from the figure

In the main visual, the cat is lying on a warmly lit wooden table, head slightly tilted, gray-brown and warm-white coat, white face and paws, large reflective eyes, a small pink nose, and a fluffy fur silhouette. The background is a blurred indoor plant and window light.

The concept board includes examples of daily actions, expressions, interaction, and times of day. These small images can serve as a mood board and as references for action intent. They do not yet have a unified camera, size, pose, and identity check, and cannot be used directly as animation keyframes or modeling orthographics.

The text in the image says “布偶猫（虚构）” (Ragdoll (fictional)), while the earlier text recommends not binding a real breed explicitly. This is kept for now as a naming difference. The main cat's appearance wins, and it is unified when the character is frozen.

## 3. Turned into design rules

| Direction | Executable rule | How to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A sense of really being there | Keep center of mass, breathing, contact, and continuous action | Watch continuously; check for floating, sliding, and sudden deformation |
| Mild neoteny | A moderately round head, a short muzzle, small paws; do not enlarge the eyes without limit | Compare candidates at the target size; avoid something that only looks good when enlarged |
| Softness | Fur silhouette, volume, and natural pressing against a surface | Check on dark and light backgrounds, not only the warm-light promotional image |
| Quiet | Rest as the main picture, a few micro-actions | Watch for 10 minutes when there is no interaction |
| Not looking posed | Do not stare at the person, smile, or pose continuously | Check whether the default state demands attention too much |
| Warmth | A restrained warm-color tendency, and a contact shadow where it is needed | Verify on a transparent desktop; avoid an overly strong orange filter |

"90% autonomous life, 10% interaction," like the other ratios in the original text, is a directional metaphor. It is not implemented as an exact probability. Autonomous behavior should have context. "Random" is not unlimited random draws of actions.

## 4. The boundary between the concept board and the first-version product

The bed, cup, windowsill, keyboard, and complete indoor space in the image strengthen the feeling that "it lives here," but a transparent desktop overlay does not have these real supports. This needs a separate check: with only the cat kept, do users still find it comfortable and natural; and whether a small mat or cat bed improves the effect without taking over the screen.

Chasing the mouse, lying on a real window, “占领键盘” (occupying the keyboard), giving gifts, and several time-of-day scenes with copy are all concept presentation. They do not automatically enter M1. In particular, “占领键盘” must not turn into blocking the user's work, and “委屈” (aggrieved) must not be used to press for care or payment.

The morning encouragement and nighttime greeting in the image can be a later reference for tone. They cannot be used to make the first version speak proactively on a schedule. “它不知道自己是 AI” (It does not know it is an AI) is kept as a fictional character premise. It is not a factual statement about system consciousness.

## 5. How far the psychological explanations may be used

The supplementary text uses Baby Schema, perceived animacy, emotional contagion, and the uncanny valley to explain the preference. This round did not check the psychology literature. They are recorded as leads for design research. They are not a basis for claiming that users will necessarily be comforted, or that a realistic-cat style necessarily causes the uncanny valley. “治愈” (soothing) in the product means a light, warm subjective experience. It is not a promise of therapeutic effect.

Later interviews should ask "why are you willing to leave this cat on the desktop" and "at which moments did you want to close it," rather than using theory to decide the reason for the user.

## 6. An optional six-candidate experiment

The supplementary material proposes A a realistic young kitten, B a 3D plush kitten, C between realistic and a figurine, D low-saturation warm light, E sleeping companionship, and F minimal realism. They change form, material, lighting, and pose at the same time. Comparing them directly makes it easy to mistake a preference for the background as a preference for the character.

The suggestion is to treat these six first as exploration labels. For a formal choice, unify frame size, camera, background, and dimensions, and compare form and material first. Then use the same chosen character to compare resting pose and lighting. Every candidate should have a small transparent-desktop preview.

If the current main image is already the one someone most wants to see every day, fill in the identity references around it directly. Do not generate six new cats just to make the process complete. This round generated no new images.
