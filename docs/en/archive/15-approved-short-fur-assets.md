# Approved short-fur direction and the accompanying materials

2026-09-12. The user explicitly approved the [short-fur kitten](../../../assets/characters/lingxi/reference/short-fur/lingxi-short-fur-v1.png) as the appearance baseline from here on. The earlier direction of long, coarse, messy hair clumps is no longer the target.

Added this round: naturally soft-pink paw pads and nose/muzzle, and a fine hazel-green iris kept at a realistic size. The white backs of the paws stay short-fur. They are not dyed pink all over.

## 36-cell form sheet

![36-cell form sheet](../../../assets/characters/lingxi/reference/short-fur/lingxi-forms-6x6-v1.png)

Top to bottom: walk and run, jumps, sitting / lying / stretching, sleep, expressions, everyday play. 6 × 6 cells in all. These are pose and expression concepts. Contact order and character-identity details still need to be calibrated in animation production.

## Material delivery

The [material-pack notes](../../../assets/characters/lingxi/materials/short-fur-v1/README.md) list the separate coat, iris, and pink soft-tissue textures, plus 8 Blender materials. The material library has the images packed. They can be brought in with Append and do not depend on image paths from the original machine.

The [material-generation evidence](../../evidence/short-fur-material-library.json) records the actual Blender assembly and render. The [reopen check](../../evidence/short-fur-material-reopen.json) records the integrity of the material library. This verification does not mean the final character appearance has reached the reference, and it does not mean UV skinning or bone weights are done.
